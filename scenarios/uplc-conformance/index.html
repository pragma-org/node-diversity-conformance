UPLC conformance

Method

Each implementation evaluates the same UPLC vectors, including the additional regression vectors maintained by the project. The resulting term or failure is compared independently from the execution budget.

Vectors without a budget expectation remain part of evaluation conformance and are excluded only from the budget denominator. The platform compares the actual outcomes with the versioned expectations for this scenario.
