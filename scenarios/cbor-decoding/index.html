CBOR decoding

Method

Each node decodes the same CDDL-generated CBOR corpus. Valid samples must decode, re-encode and match the reference bytes; invalid samples, and mutated ("zapped") ones, must be rejected. A node conforms fully when every expectation is met.

Reports follow the setup of cardano-cbor-dataset. The overall score is met expectations / total expectations, summed over the three categories and all rules. Nodes are listed under node/, each with a stable URL to its latest report; the reports are downloaded each time the site is built.
