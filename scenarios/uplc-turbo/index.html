UPLC turbo benchmarks

Method

Each implementation evaluates the same real-world UPLC corpus. The corpus was created by sampling mainnet transactions and extracting Flat-encoded programs spanning Plutus V1, V2, and V3. Flat decoding is prepared before timing and measured by a separate scenario.

The benchmark compares pure CEK evaluation time and memory growth with the expected CPU and memory budgets. An execution passes when each measured dimension is at most 100% above its corresponding budget, or within an absolute tolerance of 200 μs for CPU or 320 KiB for memory. Results within those tolerances have no delta and are excluded from the corresponding summaries and worst-case views. The report retains every script, while the compact view shows the 100 worst cases selected by the active node and ordering in a scrollable viewport.
